OUR APPROACH

More than “rare” or “vintage.”

We aim to describe each piece for what it is—not simply with labels. Instruments and gear are inspected in Japan, serviced only where appropriate, and documented so the next owner can make an informed decision.

Originality

Original parts and known replacements are identified whenever possible.

Playability

Function and practical use matter alongside collectability.

Documentation

Condition, inspection, service, and known issues are recorded clearly.

Transparency

Important details are shown, not hidden behind broad claims.

Inspection

Controls, electronics, mechanics, and relevant specifications are checked.

Appropriate servicing

Work is considered carefully, with preservation of character in mind.
